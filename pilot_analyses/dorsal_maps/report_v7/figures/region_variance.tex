The published BWM row is reproduced from the archived v7 vector panel. \textbf{a} Scores per region (Allen order and colours; bar: median; dashed: chance; grey band and line: 5--95th percentile and median of the baseline). Rows: mesoscope, one dot per session and region (149 sessions, 12 mice); BWM, one dot per probe at its entry region (175 probes); BWM decoding paper, one dot per session and region, filled if $p<0.05$ (published balanced accuracy and wheel velocity in the last column). Inset: region share from \textbf{d}. \textbf{b} Session $\times$ region scores (log $N$ removed) of a region-dominated, a balanced and a session-dominated mouse. For illustration, each matrix retains at least five regions with valid scores in every displayed session. Among equally small sets of omissions, the display keeps more regions. Horizontal stripes show session effects and vertical stripes show region effects. \textbf{c} Region minus session share from separate mixed-model fits to each mouse (11 mice with at least 4 sessions). Positive values indicate more region variance. The three example mice use the same coloured markers as in \textbf{b}. The remaining mice are shown as filled dark grey circles. \textbf{d} Shares of total variance from the pooled mesoscope mixed model. Labels give the percentage attributed to region. \textbf{e} Fraction of the remaining variance explained by region (partial $R^2$) after accounting for session and log neuron count. Black diamonds show the pooled effects (left). Coloured points show separate fits for the three mice in \textbf{b} (right). Grey bands span the central 95\% of the 9,999 within-session permutations for each fit. The pooled omnibus $p$-value across five targets is 0.0005. The individual tests are exploratory because the mice were selected as examples.}
