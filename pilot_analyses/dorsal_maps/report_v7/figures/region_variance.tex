Rows: mesoscope, one dot per session and region (149 sessions, 12 mice); BWM, one dot per probe at its entry region (175 probes); BWM decoding paper, one dot per session and region, filled if $p<0.05$ (published balanced accuracy and wheel velocity in the last column). Inset: region share from \textbf{d}. \textbf{b} Session $\times$ region scores (log $N$ removed) of a region-dominated, a balanced and a session-dominated mouse. For illustration, each matrix retains at least five regions with valid scores in every displayed session. Among equally small sets of omissions, the display keeps more regions. Horizontal stripes show session effects and vertical stripes show region effects. \textbf{c} Session minus region share from separate mixed-model fits to each mouse (11 mice with at least 4 sessions). Positive values indicate more session variance. Coloured symbols identify the example mice in \textbf{b} and \textbf{e}. \textbf{d} Shares of total variance from the pooled mesoscope mixed model. Labels give the percentage attributed to region. \textbf{e} Fraction of the remaining variance explained by region (partial $R^2$) after accounting for session and log neuron count. Circles, squares and triangles mark the example mice, and grey dots mark the other eligible mice. Black diamonds give the pooled effects. Mouse-level points are descriptive estimates. Inference uses the pooled within-session permutation test across five targets (omnibus $p=0.0005$).}
