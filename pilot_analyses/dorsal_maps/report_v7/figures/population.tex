Boxes: quartiles; whiskers: 1.5 inter-quartile ranges. \textbf{d} Same against trial-specific speed (wheel speed minus its per-bin mean over the even trials): 0.22 vs 0.19 (all neurons), 0.08 vs 0.14 (30 neurons).}
